Profile

Specialization — launch

Full product cycle: business case → technical specifications → prototypes → certification (TR CU / GOST) → pilot batch → volume production → QA/QC. I lead teams through stage-gate processes with real authority to say «stop» — before the budget burns down, not after.

Manufacturing base: own volume production in Russia and OEM/ODM partnerships in China — 30+ factory audits, direct contracts, quality control on site. Product economics is part of engineering for me: cost, pricing, margin and unit economics are computed together with the technical decisions, not after them.

«I hire people who are stronger than me in their field, give them a mandate and a safety net, and grow them through grades and mentoring. And I keep one uncomfortable habit — regularly checking that the value I create exceeds what I cost».
